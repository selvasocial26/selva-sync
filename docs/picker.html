Selva Sync

Choose the backup folder

Sign in with the same Google account you connected in Selva Sync, then choose one folder in My Drive or in a shared drive. All backups from your PC will be saved inside it.

Tip: to use a new folder, create it in Google Drive first, then choose it here.

This page only opens Google's folder chooser and sends the chosen folder's ID back to Selva Sync on your own computer. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else.
